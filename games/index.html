Games I built

Four small games of mine. I designed them, wrote the code and made the music. They run in the browser on a phone or a desktop. Free, no install, no sign-up.

Snake Rumble

Free browser snake game.

An endless neon snake arena. You don't win by being the longest snake, you win by cutting in front of rival snakes so they crash into you. Boost, pick up power-ups and stay alive while the heat goes up. The soundtrack is three of my own songs.

Play in browserPlay on Playgama

Sum Path

Free number puzzle game.

A number puzzle you play with one finger. Drag a path across touching tiles so the numbers add up to the target. It is simple to learn and gets hard quickly. There is a Zen mode with no pressure, and themes to unlock with the coins you earn.

Play in browserPlay on Playgama

Before the Paw

Free memory game with a cat.

A memory game. You get a few seconds to look at a tidy room, then the cat wrecks it. Your job is to put everything back where it was, from memory. The piano music is mine.

Play in browserPlay on Playgama

Sunken Opera

Free solo board game.

Snakes and ladders under the sea. You climb up through a drowned opera house, from the orchestra pit to the surface, with only so many breaths of air. A solo board game with painted scenes and my own music.
