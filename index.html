      Today most of my week is mixing and mastering other people's songs: indie artists, Suno and AI creators, singers, and producers who need a second pair of ears. I also release my own music, run a small independent label, Vafa Media Group, and make AI video for brands and for my own songs.

      Press bio

      Kia Vafa is a mixing and mastering engineer, producer and arranger. Trained at a music conservatory, he spent years coaching singers and playing piano for them at concerts before the studio became his main home. He writes, arranges, produces and mixes his own songs, moving between pop ballads, electronic textures and melodic lines built around a strong vocal, and mixes and masters records for independent artists, from pop and hip hop to R&B and electronic music. He founded the independent label Vafa Media Group. His latest release is "Where the Leaves Fall".
