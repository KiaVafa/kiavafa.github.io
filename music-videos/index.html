How it works

  1. Send the track

    The song, the lyrics, and a line or two about the mood. References help.

  2. I send an idea

    A short concept and a few still images, so you see the look before the video is made.

  3. I make the video

    Generated with AI, cut to the beat and graded by hand. You send notes and I finish it.

Questions

What do I need to send?

The finished song, the lyrics, and anything that shows the mood you want: a cover, a few images, another video you like.

Can the lyrics be on screen?

Yes. The Move On video on this page has the lyrics on screen.

Can you make a vertical version for Reels and TikTok?

Yes. Most of the samples here are vertical. I can deliver vertical, square and 16:9.

My song isn't mixed yet. Can you do that too?

Yes. See mixing and mastering. Doing both in one place means the video is cut to the final master.

Which AI tools do you use?

Higgsfield, Seedance, Kling and Adobe Firefly for the picture. The edit and the colour are done by hand.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the length of the video and the kind you want. Send the track and you get a clear price with the idea.
