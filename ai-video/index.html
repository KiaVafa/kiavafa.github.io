How it works

  1. Send the idea

    A script, a few lines, or just references for the mood and the look.

  2. I plan the shots

    I build the look and the characters as still images first, so faces and outfits stay the same from shot to shot.

  3. I make the film

    Generate, cut, grade and score. You see it, send notes, and I finish it.

Questions

Which AI video tools do you use?

Higgsfield, Seedance, Kling and Adobe Firefly for the picture, and LLMs like Claude for scripts and planning. I cut and grade in Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve.

Can you keep the same character in every shot?

Yes. I design the character as still images first and use them as the reference for every shot.

What formats can I get?

Vertical for Reels, Shorts and TikTok, square, or 16:9.

Do you make the music too?

Yes. I'm a music producer and mix engineer, so the score and the sound are made for the picture.

Do you make ads and music videos as well?

Yes. See AI video ads and AI music videos.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the length and the number of shots. Send the idea and you get a clear price before any work starts.

Got a story that needs a picture?

Send me the idea or the script. I'll come back with how I'd shoot it.
