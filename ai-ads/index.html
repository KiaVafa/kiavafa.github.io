Questions

What do you need from me?

Clear photos of the product from a few angles, your logo, one line about who the ad is for, and where it will run. That is enough to start.

How long can the ad be?

Most of what I make is between 10 and 30 seconds, which is what works on Reels, Shorts and TikTok.

Will my product look right?

I use your own product photos as the reference. AI can drift on small text and logos, so I check every shot and redo or fix the ones that are off.

Which AI tools do you use?

Higgsfield, Seedance, Kling and Adobe Firefly for the picture, and Claude for scripts and planning. The edit, the grade and the music are done by hand.

Can you make a series for my brand?

Yes. Brand Versus on this page was made as a sample for brands that want a series.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the length and how many shots the idea needs. Send the brief and you get a clear price with the idea, before any work starts.
