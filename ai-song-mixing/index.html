What I do to the track

  • Stems

    If you only have one finished file, I separate it into vocal, drums, bass and instruments first.

  • Cleanup

    The metallic shimmer, the smeared vocal and the noise that AI tracks tend to carry.

  • Mix

    A real mix on the separated parts: a clear vocal, a low end that holds, and space around the instruments.

  • Master

    A streaming-ready master at a level that matches other songs in a playlist.

  • 3 revisions

    You listen, send notes, and I change it.

Questions

I only have one MP3 or WAV. Is that enough?

Yes. I separate it into stems myself. A WAV is better than an MP3 if the tool lets you download one.

Can you make an AI song sound like a studio recording?

I can make it a lot cleaner, fuller and louder, and most people hear the difference right away. Some artifacts are baked into the file and cannot be removed completely. I will tell you honestly what to expect after I hear it.

Can you change the song itself?

Small things, yes: tightening an intro, cutting a section, fixing an ending. For new parts or a new arrangement, look at music production.

Which AI tools do you work with?

Suno mostly, and songs from other AI music tools too. What matters to me is the audio file, not where it came from.
