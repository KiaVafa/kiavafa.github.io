Music production & arrangement

I'm a musician first. I trained at a music conservatory, started on piano and spent years coaching singers and playing behind them at concerts. Now I arrange, produce and mix songs, my own and other people's.

What I can do for your song

  • Arrangement

    Your melody and chords turned into a full song: structure, instruments, and the parts that make a chorus lift.

  • Production

    Live-feel production for pop songs built around a vocal, from the first sketch to the final mix.

  • Ghost production

    Melodic house and techno, produced to your reference. The track is fully yours.

  • Mixing included

Listen to my own releases

I have released 27 tracks since 2023 on my own label, Vafa Media Group. I wrote, arranged, produced and mixed these myself. The full catalogue is on Spotify and YouTube.

How it works

  1. Send what you have

    A voice memo, a demo, lyrics with chords, or a reference track for ghost production.

  2. I build the song

    I send you a first version to react to, and tell you up front how long it will take.

  3. We finish it

    You send notes, I revise, then I mix and master the final version.

Questions

What do I need to send?

Anything that carries the idea: a phone recording of you singing, a rough demo, or just lyrics and chords. One or two reference songs tell me the sound you want.

Who owns a ghost-produced track?

You do. It is produced to your reference and it is fully yours to release under your name.

Do you also mix and master the song?

Yes. A production from me comes back mixed and mastered.

Which styles do you produce?

Pop and electronic music, especially songs built around a strong vocal, and melodic house and techno for ghost production.

Can you write a piano or keys part for my song?

Yes. Piano is my first instrument.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how much there is to build. Send the idea and you get a clear price before any work starts.

Got a song that only exists as an idea?

Send me the demo or the reference. I'll listen and tell you how I'd build it.
