How it works

  1. Send the song

    A link or your stems. I listen and tell you what I would do before you pay anything.

  2. I mix and master

    Most songs are done in 3 to 5 days. I work in Cubase and Ableton with UAD plugins and tools.

  3. You send notes

    Listen on your own speakers and tell me what to change. Three revisions are included.

Questions

What do I need to send?

Export every track as its own WAV file, all starting from the same point, with nothing on the master channel. A rough mix and one or two reference songs help me understand what you are going for.

How long does a mix take?

Usually 3 to 5 days for a song. If you have a release date, tell me at the start.

How many revisions do I get?

Three rounds of changes are included with every mix.

Can you master a song that is already mixed?

Yes. Send the stereo mix without a limiter on it and I will master it. If something in the mix holds the master back, I will tell you before I start.

Which genres do you work on?

Mostly pop, hip hop, R&B and electronic music, including melodic house. If a song is built around a voice, I am at home.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the song and how many tracks it has. Send it over and you get a clear price before any work starts. Listening is free.

Do you fix songs made with Suno or other AI tools?

Yes, a lot of them. There is a separate page about that: Suno and AI song mixing.

Got a song that isn't there yet?
